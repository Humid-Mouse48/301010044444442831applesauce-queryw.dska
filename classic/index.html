How to play Classic

  1. Arch and tuck. Hold Arch to shape your swing, or Tuck to pull in. You can hold multiple on-screen buttons together.
  2. Release and twist. Press Release / twist to let go of the bar. Press it again in the air to twist.
  3. Regrab. Let go of the Release / twist button. The original game logic grabs automatically when the hands meet its grip conditions; there is no separate catch button.
  4. Try again. Reset returns to the selected spawn. Menu lets you choose a different starting position without leaving Classic.
  5. On a phone. Use Expand, turn the phone sideways, and try the larger buttons or left-handed layout in Menu. Lifting your finger or opening a menu releases touch controls.
  6. On a keyboard. Click the game to focus it. Current keys appear below the touch buttons; change them in Settings and keys.
